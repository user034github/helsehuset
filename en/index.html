Our team

  • Dr. Kjell Vaage

    Founder – general practitioner

    Kjell was educated in Germany. He was the owner of and directed Greveløkka Medisinske Senter, established Legegruppen Danmarksplass (w/ ca. 30,000 consultations pr. year). He established Pondusklinikken (overweight clinic), was Medical founder and board member at World Medical Card (2003-2012), and established this company in Gran Canaria (2007).

  • Dr. Ivan Jovovic

    Specialist in general medicine and community medicine

    Ivan has 22 years of experience from working as a GP, in the ER, and at hospitals (internal medicine/geriatrics; experience from radiology, anesthesiology, and emergency medicine). Shifts at Sørlandet hospital and Sirdal municipality. Stavanger ER (3 years). Experience from Aker and Namsos hospital. Chief Municipal Medical Officer in Sirdal. Supervisor LIS1/ALIS, and former university lecturer at University of Bergen (2013-2019).

  • Dr. Jon Vangdal Aamaas

    Specialist in general medicine and community medicine

    Jon was educated at University of Bergen (1999–2006). He has broad experience from general medicine and ER, nursing home medicine, geriatric psychiatry and private health service. He has over 20 years of combined experience as GP in the Bergen area, Chief Municipal Medical Officer and senior medical consultant at NKS Olaviken with specialized expertise in cognitive examination and dementia treatment.

  • Professor Glenn Haugeberg

    Specialist in rheumatology

    Glenn er utdannet i Hamburg, med doktorgrad i Tyskland (1990) og Norge (2003), og er en av Norges mest publiserte revmatologer. Han jobbet i klinikk på Sørlandet (tidl. seksjonsleder), Martina Hansens, St. Olavs og Diakonhjemmet. Han er tidligere professor ved NTNU; gjesteforsker i USA, England og Polen.

  • Heart specialist Egil Vaage

    Cardiologist

    Egil har bakgrunn fra SUS (medisinsk/kardiologisk) og hospitering ved Sahlgrenska. Han er grunnlegger og tidligere leder av Stavanger Hjertesenter AS (1993–2021). ESC-engasjement; foredrag nasjonalt og internasjonalt. Han er forfatter av pasienthefter; undervisning for sykepleierutdanningen.

  • Physio- and manual therapist Morten Skjesol

    Physio- and manual therapist

    Morten er en av Norges mest erfarne manuellterapeuter og idrettsfysioterapeuter. Han har 30+ år i privat praksis og arbeidet for flere norske landslag. Videreutdanning i manuellterapi, idrettsmedisin, injeksjoner og akupunktur. Han er gründer av ANT HMS & Bedriftshelse AS (m/ ca. 15,000 medlemmer).

Common questions

Do I have to make an appointment or can I walk-in?▼

You don't have to make an appointment, but there could be waiting times without an appointment.

Is parking free for visitors?▼

Yes, parking is free for all our visitors.

How does insurance work?▼

We cooperate with all Norwegian insurance companies, and many Swedish and Danish ones. We help with the necessary documentation and communication with the insurance company or alarm central. When there is a payment guarantee, the treatment can in many cases be handled directly through travel insurance.

Can I reschedule or cancel an appointment that I've made?▼

Yes, please call us at +34 92 898 8030.

Can I book a phone consultation?▼

Yes, and you may do that by booking an appointment as usual, and then calling us at the appointed time instead of coming to the office. You may call us at +34 92 898 8030.
